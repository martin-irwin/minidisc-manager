Important information - please read

1. Back up first.
   MiniDisc Manager can write to and erase MiniDiscs. It has only been tried on a Sony MZ-NH700 and an MZ-RH1, and a disc can be
   left unplayable if a write is interrupted. Copy any recordings you cannot replace to your PC before you edit a disc, and do not
   unplug the recorder while the program says it is working.

2. A USB driver step is needed, once per recorder.
   The program talks to the recorder directly over USB, which needs the WinUSB driver. At the end of this setup you can open the
   driver setup guide (it is also in the Start Menu under MiniDisc Manager). It takes about two minutes with a free tool called Zadig.
   Until it is done the program will say "No recorder found".

3. Windows may ask about network access.
   The first time you play to a Sonos speaker or a Google TV, Windows Firewall may ask. Choose "Allow" for private networks.

4. Your data stays on your PC.
   Backups of each disc's track index and a small log are kept in %LOCALAPPDATA%\MiniDiscManager. Uninstalling does not delete them.

5. Not affiliated.
   MiniDisc Manager is not affiliated with or endorsed by Sony or Sonos. It is free software built on open-source libraries; the licences
   are in the install folder (LICENSE.txt, THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt and the LICENSES folder).
